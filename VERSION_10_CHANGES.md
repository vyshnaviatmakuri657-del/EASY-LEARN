# EASY-LEARN v10

## Exam Pulse

Added a new Exam Pulse study-planning tab to the v9 project.

- Student selects an exam date.
- Remaining days are calculated automatically.
- Important topics are loaded from the uploaded document.
- Higher-importance topics are scheduled earlier.
- Final day is reserved for revision and exam readiness.
- Shows estimated study time, topics to master, study activities, a timeline, and a topic constellation.
- Exam date is saved in localStorage for the current browser.
- Existing chatbot, RAG, study tabs, and v9 upload animation are preserved.
